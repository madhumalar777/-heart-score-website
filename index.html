GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How long does the process take?+

Most songs take 4–6 weeks from your first questionnaire to final delivery, depending on the package and revisions.

What if we don't like the first draft?+

Every package includes revision rounds. Tell us exactly what to change — a line, a verse, the mood — and we'll refine it until it feels right.

Can we use the song commercially?+

Yes — once delivered, the song is yours to use for your wedding film, reels and personal keepsakes.

Do you work with couples outside India?+

Yes, the entire process runs over WhatsApp, email and video calls, so distance isn't a barrier.

What if we're not musical at all?+

That's the point of Heart Score — you bring the story, we bring the music. No musical background needed.
